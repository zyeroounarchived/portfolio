About — what I actually do

I'm a first‑year Business Analytics student at ITM Skills University. Most of my time lately is split between coursework, an internship where I handled real applicant data for an NGO, and small studio projects where I practise turning messy inputs into something someone can actually use. I prefer work that earns its keep: clear data, simple reproducible steps, and a short write-up that a non-technical person can follow.

How I work: I start by asking why the data exists and who needs the answer. Then I standardise columns, fix encodings, handle edge cases, and document the assumptions. My usual deliverables are a cleaned dataset, a short codebook, a reproducible notebook or sheet, and one slide with the top recommendation.

Tools & learning: Python for ETL and small scripts, MySQL/SQLite for tidy storage, R for quick stats, and Excel for rapid prototyping. Right now I'm improving my testing and reproducibility habits so analyses survive edits. Outside work I shoot photos, write short pieces, and play basketball — things that keep projects feeling human.

— Nabhya
